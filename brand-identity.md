# Scopwise Brand Identity

## Brand Core

### Name

**Scopwise**

The name combines scope with wise judgment. It suggests visibility, boundaries, and informed review without sounding like a cybersecurity utility or generic AI product.

### Category

Agent access governance for enterprise AI.

### Brand promise

Scopwise makes every agent’s access visible, controllable, and provable.

### Primary line

**Boundaries people can see.**

### Supporting line

Know what every agent can access. Control it. Prove it.

### Brand idea

**The evidence atlas.**

Enterprise agent access is usually invisible, fragmented, and difficult to explain. Scopwise turns it into a legible map of agents, systems, actions, owners, rules, decisions, and evidence.

## Brand Personality

- Precise without feeling cold
- Responsible without sounding bureaucratic
- Creative without becoming playful infrastructure theater
- European and privacy-aware without relying on flags or regulatory cliches
- Confident without making absolute security claims
- Technical enough for platform teams and clear enough for business reviewers

## Voice

### Tone

Calm, direct, observant, and evidence-led. Scopwise speaks like an experienced reviewer who can make complex systems understandable.

### Preferred verbs

Map, reveal, define, constrain, trace, compare, review, approve, explain, prove.

### Preferred language

- “See the complete access path.”
- “Define the boundary before deployment.”
- “Review every change with evidence.”
- “Know who owns the decision.”
- “Bring agents to review with complete context.”

### Avoid

- Revolutionary, magical, autonomous, intelligent, seamless, or future-proof
- Fear-heavy cybersecurity language
- “Total security,” “zero risk,” or unsupported compliance claims
- Generic “AI governance for the future” messaging
- Excessive acronyms before the problem is understood

## Selected Visual System

The visual identity combines three related expressions under one system.

### Prism Ledger

Primary homepage and brand expression.

- Pale blue-white or warm-white canvas
- Horizontal pastel bands
- A central access boundary or review axis
- Restrained, left-aligned editorial headline
- Visible progression from Agent to Data, App, Action, Owner, and Review
- Lavender as the principal expressive accent

### Evidence Garden

Map, product discovery, integrations, and visibility pages.

- Fine access routes and connected node families
- Pastel colors distinguish agents or semantic object types
- Assumed-access versus actual-access comparisons
- Detailed ownership and review columns
- Higher information density while preserving generous spacing

### Signal Atelier

Review, trust, security, company, and conversion sections.

- Warm paper surface
- Apricot for attention and exceptions
- Mint for approved and verified states
- Human ownership and timestamps
- Evidence journal and review-table compositions
- Editorial serif accents in headlines and pull quotes

## Logo System

### Selected concept: Scoped S

The selected symbol uses three offset evidence layers for Map, Rules, and Review. A continuous `S`-shaped negative-space route passes through the layers, representing agent access moving from discovery to control and finally to provable review.

The mark is geometric, compact, and recognizable at favicon size. It avoids shields, locks, targets, AI sparkles, and generic network icons.

Production SVG assets are stored in `public/brand/`, with the website favicon at `public/favicon.svg`.

### Wordmark

- `Scopwise` set in Manrope Medium
- Custom optical adjustment between `p`, `w`, and `i`
- Capital `S`, lowercase remaining characters
- Letter spacing `0`
- Symbol-to-wordmark gap: `16 px`

### Required lockups

- Primary horizontal: symbol plus wordmark
- Wordmark-only
- Compact symbol
- Reverse white-on-ink
- Monochrome black
- Favicon at 32 px and 16 px

### Clear space

Minimum clear space equals the height of the lowercase `o` around all sides of the lockup.

### Minimum sizes

- Horizontal digital lockup: 112 px wide
- Symbol: 20 px for interface use
- Favicon: test separately at 16 px and 32 px

## Typography

Exactly two Google Font families are used.

### Manrope

The structural and product typeface.

- Navigation
- Product UI
- Buttons and forms
- Body copy
- Labels and metadata
- The non-italic portion of display headlines

Default weights are Regular and Medium. Semibold is reserved for labels, buttons, and compact interface emphasis. Bold and ExtraBold are not part of the normal brand system.

### Instrument Serif

The editorial accent typeface.

- Short italic headline phrases
- Pull quotes
- Section transitions
- Brand storytelling

It should never carry long paragraphs, navigation, form labels, or dense product data.

## Layout and Spacing

### Desktop grid

- Reference viewport: `1440 px`
- Content width: `1312 px`
- Outer margins: `64 px`
- Columns: 12
- Gutters: `24 px`
- Navbar height: `80 px`
- Long-form copy: approximately 5-6 columns

### Spacing scale

Only `4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96 px`.

- Standard section padding: `96 px`
- Heading to body: `24 px`
- Body to action: `32 px`
- Section heading to section content: `48 px`
- Product and diagram gaps: `24 px`
- Major storyline interval: `96 px`

### Shape

- Cards: maximum `8 px` radius
- Buttons: `8 px` radius
- Inputs: `6-8 px` radius
- State pills: `8 px` radius, never excessively capsule-shaped
- Shadows: subtle and reserved for overlays or essential evidence elevation

## Graphic Language

### Core objects

- Agent
- Data
- Application
- Action
- Owner
- Rule
- Change
- Decision
- Evidence
- Review

### Access routes

- Thin 1-2 px paths
- Solid lines for confirmed access
- Dashed lines for inherited, inferred, or incomplete relationships
- Pastel route color identifies a semantic group or agent family
- Intersections and boundary crossings are explicitly marked
- Animation follows the path from cause to consequence

### Evidence records

Evidence components should expose:

- What changed
- Which agent is affected
- What system or action is involved
- Who owns the review
- The decision
- Exceptions or conditions
- Evidence source
- Timestamp

## Iconography

Use Lucide icons in the implemented product and website.

- Default stroke: 1.5-2 px
- Default sizes: 16, 20, or 24 px
- Icons support labels; they do not replace important state language
- Avoid custom shield, lock, robot, sparkle, or brain icons

## Photography and Illustration

The primary brand does not depend on stock photography. Product diagrams, access maps, evidence records, and selective founder or customer photography should carry the site.

When photography is introduced:

- Use real founders, team members, customers, or operating environments
- Prefer documentary light and natural color
- Avoid generic office teams, holograms, server rooms, and staged cybersecurity imagery

## Motion

Motion explains the sequence:

1. Map reveals the real relationship.
2. Rules apply a visible boundary.
3. Review records the decision and evidence.

Use restrained line drawing, state transitions, sticky chapters, and horizontal progression. Avoid elastic motion, decorative particle fields, constant floating, or animation that obscures product meaning.

2. **You can’t govern what you can’t see.** Evidence Garden Map reveal.
3. **Clear rules, applied in real time.** Policy bands and action states.
4. **Every change leaves evidence.** Signal Atelier review table.
5. **Bring your next agent to review with evidence.** Warm conversion band.

## Governance

- Do not add new colors outside the defined system without design review.
- Do not introduce a third font family.
- Do not use extra-bold or condensed display fonts.
- Do not use gradients as the primary visual device.
- Do not use unverified customer logos, certifications, compliance claims, or statistics.
- Maintain the shared spacing scale, 12-column grid, icon system, and semantic colors across every page.
